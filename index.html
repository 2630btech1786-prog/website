Protect Our Environment

The environment gives us air, water, food, and shelter. It is our responsibility to keep our planet clean and green.

Why Is the Environment Important?

Trees, plants, animals, and clean water are essential for life. Pollution and waste harm our planet. Small steps can make a big difference.

Simple Eco-Friendly Tips

🌱 Plant Trees

Plant more trees to keep the air clean.

♻️ Reduce Waste

Reuse items and recycle paper, plastic, and glass.

💧 Save Water

Turn off taps when water is not needed.

💡 Save Energy

Switch off lights and fans when leaving a room.
